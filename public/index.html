Census of Hands

Write a digit, and meet the people in the MNIST archive who write it like you.

On the iPad

  • Pen — the writing surface
  • Collect — write training samples

On the Mac

  • Census of Hands (mouse) — draw on the Mac, no iPad: the archive's digits gather around yours, by handwriting
  • Census of Hands — the piece: meet the 12 people who write like you
  • Display — the earlier music version
  • Review — check and clean samples
  • Train — train and try the recogniser

Piano: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC-BY 3.0). Digits and their writers from QMNIST (Yadav & Bottou, 2019; BSD licence), the reconstructed MNIST training set.
